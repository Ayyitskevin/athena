- **A slice shows the work order it inherits.** When an issue's body lacks any
  of the six work-order parts and it has a parent, its work context (full and
  brief) carries `work_order.inherited`: the nearest visible ancestor's goal,
  success criteria and other parts the slice lacks, with their text and who
  wrote it. A seat working a slice reads the acceptance criteria it is held to
  without reading the outcome by hand.
