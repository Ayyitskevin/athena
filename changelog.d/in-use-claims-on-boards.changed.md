- **Boards and the issue list say who holds the live claim (U2).** A board
  card names the holder of its live lease beside the assignee, and its lane
  follows whoever is working it: an unassigned issue an agent has claimed is
  under "Agent work", where it sat under "Unassigned". `?group=claim` (the
  "Lanes" select) gives one lane per claim holder. The issue list gains a
  Claim column.
