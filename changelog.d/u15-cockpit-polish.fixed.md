- **The cockpit obeys its own design system (U15).** No page scrolls sideways at
  390 px or 1280 px with a 120-character name in it (before, every page did at
  390 px): the navigation wraps, every table scrolls inside its own scroller,
  long names break instead of widening their box, and selects shrink. The
  default button is ink rather than the lime that means "an agent is waiting on
  you". Every page opens with a "Skip to content" link. Route paths, "data from
  the API" footers and REST instructions are gone from the interface, and an
  empty page says what to do next, with a link for whoever may do it.
